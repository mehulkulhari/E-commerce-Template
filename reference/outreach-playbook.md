## 0) The one line that sells (memorise this)
You are **not** "a website guy." You say:

> "I help Instagram boutiques stop losing sales in the DMs — I turn your page into a store where customers see everything and order on WhatsApp in two taps."

Sell the **outcome** (more orders, less DM chaos), never "a website."

---

## 1) Who to target first (your list of 100)
Start narrow: **women's clothing boutiques on Instagram** — that's what your template and sample already fit. You'll reuse the same pitch and design, so every next client gets faster.

**How to build a list of 100 in ~2 hours:**
1. On Instagram, search hashtags: `#jodhpurboutique` `#jodhpurfashion` `#rajasthanboutique` `#jaipurboutique` `#indianboutiqueonline` `#boutiqueindia`.
2. Also try Google Maps: "boutique Jodhpur", "designer suits Jodhpur" — many have IG links.
3. Add anyone who fits **these green flags**:
   - Posts regularly (active in last 7 days)
   - **500–20,000 followers** (big enough to afford you, small enough to still reply personally)
   - Comments full of "price?" / "DM" → *this is your golden signal — they're drowning in exactly the problem you fix*
   - Only a Linktree or no link in bio
4. Put them in a simple sheet: `Handle | Followers | Notable (e.g. lots of price DMs) | Status | Date`.

**Skip** (red flags): pages that already have a proper website linked, huge brands, dead pages (no post in a month), or accounts that clearly won't pay (giveaway/repost pages).

## 2) The offer that does the converting: the FREE PREVIEW
Don't sell the website. Offer a **free 60-second preview**: a short screen-recording (or 2–3 screenshots) showing *their* products in your template. It feels custom, costs you 15 minutes, and flips the conversation from "should I?" to "how much?"

**How to make one fast:**
1. Clone your `boutique-template.html`, swap 3–4 of their real product photos + prices in.
2. Screen-record scrolling through it on your phone (any screen recorder), or take clean screenshots.
3. Send it with the script below.

Do **2–3 free previews a day** for your first two weeks. This is the whole game early on.

## 3) The DM scripts

### Opener — Hinglish (use for most Jodhpur/Rajasthan boutiques)
> Hi [Name] 👋 Aapki page ki collection bahut sundar hai — especially the [specific item, e.g. anarkali sets] 😍
> Ek baat notice ki — comments me bahut saare "price?" aur "DM" aate hain. Main chhoti boutiques ke liye simple websites banata hoon jahan customer sab kuch dekh kar seedha WhatsApp pe order kar sakte hain (2 tap me).
> Aapke liye ek **free demo** bana doon apni page ki photos se? Bilkul free, no pressure — bas dekhna kaisa lagta hai. 🙂

### Opener — English (for pan-India / English-first pages)
> Hi [Name]! Your collection is gorgeous — the [specific item] especially 😍
> I noticed your comments are full of "price?" and "DM" — I build simple websites for boutiques so customers can see everything and order on WhatsApp in 2 taps, instead of you replying to each DM.
> Want me to make you a **free demo** using your own products? No charge, no obligation — just so you can see it. 🙂

**Rules for the opener:**
- Always name **one specific product** you actually saw. Generic = ignored.
- Keep it short. No price, no link in the first message. Goal = get a "yes, show me."
- Send 15–20 a day, spaced out (avoid Instagram's spam limits — don't blast 50 identical messages in 10 minutes).

### When they say "yes, show me" → send the preview
> Awesome 🙌 Here's a quick demo I made with your products: [video/screenshots]
> Aur ye ek live sample hai taaki idea aa jaaye: [sample store link]
> Customers just tap a product → WhatsApp opens with the order ready. Want me to tell you how it works and the pricing?

### When they ask "price?" / "kitne ka hai?"
> For a page like yours, most boutiques go with my **Boutique package — ₹8,999 one-time**: up to 40 products, categories, your own domain, and it shows up on Google too.
> If you want to start smaller, there's a **Starter at ₹2,999**. Both are one-time, no monthly fees (a small ₹799/month care plan is optional if you want me to keep updating it).
> I take 50% to start and it's ready in 3–4 days. Shall I send you the full details? [sales page link]

### Follow-ups (most sales are in the follow-up — don't skip these)
- **Follow-up 1 (after 2 days, no reply):**
  > Hi [Name], just floating this back up 🙂 Should I go ahead and make that free demo for your page? Takes me 15 mins, costs you nothing.
- **Follow-up 2 (after 3–4 more days):**
  > No worries if now's not the time! I'll leave it here — whenever you want customers to order without the DM back-and-forth, just message me 🙂
- Then **stop.** Two follow-ups max. Move on.

## 4) Objection handling (short answers)
- **"I already have Instagram / I don't need a website."**
  > Totally — Instagram is great for getting seen. This is for *selling*: one link where people order instantly instead of DMing. It works alongside your page, not instead of it.
- **"Too expensive."**
  > I get it — that's why there's a ₹2,999 Starter. Think of it this way: it pays for itself in 2–3 orders, and then it keeps selling for free. Want to start there?
- **"I'll think about it."**
  > Of course! Can I at least send the free demo so you have something concrete to think about? No obligation.
- **"Can you do it cheaper?"**
  > My prices are already set for small businesses, so I keep them fixed to be fair to everyone. But we can start with a smaller package and upgrade later.
  *(Don't cave on price — discount by reducing scope, e.g. Starter instead, not by dropping the number.)*
- **"Will it be difficult to manage?"**
  > Nope — you just WhatsApp me photos and prices, I do everything technical. And the ₹799 care plan means I handle updates for you.

## 5) Pricing cheat-sheet
| Package | Price | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | ₹2,999 one-time | First-timers, small catalogue (≤12 products), link-in-bio replacement |
| **Boutique** ⭐ | ₹8,999 one-time | Your default pitch — full catalogue, domain, Google, reviews |
| **Store** | ₹24,999+ | Bigger sellers who want online payments + cart |
| **Care Plan** | ₹799/month | Optional add-on: hosting + monthly updates |

- **Always take 50% advance.** No exceptions early on.
- Domain (₹800–1,200/yr) + hosting billed at cost, or bundle into the Care Plan.
- Push the **Care Plan** — recurring ₹799/month from 20 clients = ₹15,980/month before you build anything new. That's what turns this from a hustle into a business.

## 6) Your first-week plan (day by day)
**Day 1 — Set up (2–3 hrs)**
- Edit both HTML files: your WhatsApp number, Instagram, email. Re-publish.
- Save the sample-store link and sales-page link in your phone's Notes for easy pasting.
- Create a business Instagram (or use your own) with a clear bio: *"Websites for Instagram boutiques 📍Jodhpur | Order on WhatsApp ↓"* + your sales-page link.

**Day 2 — Build your list (2 hrs)**
- Find and log **50 boutiques** using the hashtags in section 1.

**Day 3 — First outreach (1.5 hrs)**
- DM **15–20** from your list with the opener. Log who you messaged.
- Make **1 free preview** for the most promising one.

**Day 4 — Preview + follow-up (1.5 hrs)**
- Send previews to anyone who replied "yes."
- DM **15–20 new** boutiques.
- Add **25 more** to your list.

**Day 5 — Push for a call/close (1.5 hrs)**
- Follow up (Follow-up 1) with everyone from Day 3 who didn't reply.
- Send pricing to anyone warm.
- DM **15–20 new**.

**Day 6 — Convert (1 hr)**
- Answer questions, send the sales page, ask for the 50% advance from anyone ready.
- Offer: *"I've got space for 2 builds this week — want one of them?"* (gentle urgency, and it's true).

**Day 7 — Review & restock**
- Count your numbers (below). Restock your list to 100. Rest.

By end of week 1, realistic targets: **~70 DMs sent, 15–25 replies, 5–10 previews, 1–3 people asking price, 0–2 paid.** The first "yes" is the hardest. It speeds up fast after.

---

## 7) Numbers to track (one line a day)
`Date | DMs sent | Replies | Previews made | Price asked | Advance received`

If replies are low → your opener isn't specific enough (name a real product).
If replies but no price-asks → your preview isn't landing (make it more custom).
If price-asks but no sales → practice the objection answers + always ask for the advance.

## 8) Free tools you need
- **Instagram** — outreach + your own portfolio page.
- **A screen recorder** (built into most phones) — for free previews.
- **Google Sheets / Notes** — your lead list.
- **Canva (free)** — quick before/after graphics to post.
- **Netlify / Vercel / GitHub Pages (free tiers)** — to host client sites cheaply; or Hostinger (~₹149/mo) for a India-friendly paid option with easy domains.
- **Razorpay** — when you get to Store clients who need online payments.

---

## 9) When to say NO (protect yourself)
- Client won't pay any advance → walk away.
- "Just make it first, I'll pay if I like it" → no. Free preview only, never a free full build.
- Endless "one more change" with no scope → that's what the Care Plan and written scope are for. Put "what's included" in writing before you start.
- Someone who beats you down on every rupee → they'll be your most painful client. Politely pass.

---

## 10) The mindset
The first 2–3 clients are the hard part — do a couple cheap or even one free (for a testimonial + permission to show it) to build proof. After that, **referrals and your growing portfolio do the selling.** Niche down, stay consistent with daily DMs, and always add the recurring care plan. That's the whole business.
