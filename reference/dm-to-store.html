Questions

Good to know.

I already have Instagram. Why do I need this?

Instagram gets you seen; it doesn’t help people buy. A site gives customers one place to browse everything and order instantly — so you stop losing buyers who won’t wait for a DM reply.

Will my customers have to learn something new?

No. They still order on WhatsApp — the app they already use. The site just makes it faster and clearer, so more of them actually complete the order.

How long does it take?

Starter is usually ready in about 2 days, Boutique in 3–4. I’ll give you an exact date before we start.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. You send me your product photos and prices over WhatsApp; I handle the domain, hosting, and every technical part.

What if I want changes later?

Small tweaks are covered by the Care Plan (₹799/month). Bigger changes are quoted upfront so there are never surprise bills.

How do payments work?

On Starter and Boutique, customers order via WhatsApp and pay you how you already accept (UPI, COD, etc.). The Store package adds automatic online payments — UPI and cards — on the site itself.
